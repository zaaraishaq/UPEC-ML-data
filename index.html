Multi-Locus Sequence Typing clusters clonal lineages in UPEC, but the accessory elements driving antibiotic cross-survival parameters are dynamic. We trained classification arrays over integrated predictor profiles, successfully down-filtering inputs to 12,012 highly significant biomarkers using Benjamini-Hochberg FDR filtering constraints (padj < 0.005) across 17 distinct sequence types.
