Bioinformatics & Machine Learning Pipeline

Step 1

Cohort Curation

1,186 genomes imported; 2 duplicate assemblies (MG655_, CFT073_) removed. 1,184 clean genomes retained.

Step 2

Predictive Matrix

75,068 binary genomic predictors integrated (combining 72,590 pangenomic clusters and 2,594 specific annotated features).

Step 3

FDR-Filter

Cohort narrowed to 17 STs with ≥10 representatives (838 isolates). Filtered features to **12,012 highly significant markers** via Chi-Square BH-FDR correction ($p_{adj} < 0.005$).

Step 4

Classification Models

Trained and validated 8 distinct classifiers across 12,012 markers using Stratified 5-Fold Cross-Validation.

Key Synergy Clusters

Hemolysin & CNF1 Island r = 0.94

Alpha-hemolysin core operon (hlyA, hlyC, hlyD) displays high co-occurrence with cytotoxic necrotizing factor 1 (cnf1) on Pathogenicity Island IICFT073, causing mucosal cell exfoliation during UTI pathogenesis.

CTX-M-15 ESBL Mobilization r = 0.88

The extended-spectrum beta-lactamase gene blaCTX-M-15 is strongly linked with the insertion element ISEcp1, which mediates transposition events, and aminoglycoside resistance elements.

Yersiniabactin Siderophore r = 0.92

High-affinity iron chelating components irp1, irp2 and their receptor fyuA form an absolute functional block. Yersiniabactin scavenger genes are crucial for fitness in iron-limited urine environments.

Plasmid Replicon Vectors (MGE) r = 0.89

IncFII and IncFIB plasmid replicons behave as central network hubs in MDR clones (such as ST131, ST1193, and ST648), carrying multi-drug resistance transposons and integrons (intI1) as single physical units.

Study Synopsis & Objectives

Multi-Locus Sequence Typing (MLST) clusters clonal lineages in UPEC, but the precise accessory genes driving lineage fitness and antibiotic survival are highly dynamic. We designed a machine learning pipeline trained on 75,068 binary genomic predictors computed across 1,184 global isolates, which we reduced via statistical evaluation and BH-FDR correction ($p_{adj} < 0.005$) to **12,012 highly significant biomarkers**—integrating MGE plasmid replicons, insertion elements, and integrases along with virulence and resistance cassettes across the 838 high-confidence genomes. Gradient Boosting classification on this refined dataset achieves an accuracy of ~98.8%, pinpointing critical lineages-specific markers driving global epidemic success.
